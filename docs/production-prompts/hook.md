original hook to the new subject. Refine wording in the persona's voice within that relationship, keeping
what the source foregrounds, its reader question or promise, and how the body answers it. Compare alternatives
within this framing and retain the current version when stronger. Do not independently replace the framing
with a broader benefit, urgency or curiosity angle merely because it sounds stronger. Preserve enough
specificity for the cover to identify the subject and the kind of answer the reader will receive.

original hook from the new draft or recollect external posts. Record the observed relationship in review.

qualifications being used to force a connection? Repair a mismatch using the original relationship and
