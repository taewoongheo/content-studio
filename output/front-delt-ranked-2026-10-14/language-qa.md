## Changes

| Original | Final | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Find the exercise that fits your training. | Pick one for your next shoulder workout. | Concrete next action, rather than a generic promise. |
| Direct front-delt work with little setup. | A simple way to train your front delts. | Natural sentence rather than abstract noun-heavy phrasing. |
| Small jumps in difficulty are harder to dial in. | It's harder to increase the load in small steps. | State the actual progression problem in everyday English. |
| A stable press with less balancing to manage. | The fixed bar path makes balancing easier. | Clear subject and mechanism; removes the awkward “balancing to manage.” |
| A strong option for adding weight over time. | You can add weight in small steps. | Removes generic praise and names the practical benefit. |
| Heavy reps demand solid bracing and bar control. | Keep your torso steady and the bar under control. | Plain cue instead of formal coaching jargon. |

Hook and exercise names retained. Dumbbell and machine descriptions retained because they are already natural and specific. Project title corrected to Build Bigger Shoulders - 6 Exercises, Ranked; the previous name still said seven.
